Why most people wait — and why it's a false economy. Booking privately today costs about £17 more than an NHS checkup (£45 private vs £27.90 NHS Band 1). That small saving is exactly why most people choose to wait. But if decay is missed and allowed to progress, the eventual cost of treating it — a filling that's grown into a crown, or worse, an extraction and a denture or implant — is often 10–50× higher than what you'd save by waiting.
